RTRD Inventory Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

RTRD Inventory is an internal inventory operations application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when an authorized eBay seller connects an eBay account to the application.

Information we access

With the seller’s permission, RTRD Inventory may access eBay inventory and offer information, including seller-defined SKUs, product details, listing status, image references, and limited account information needed to provide the service. The application receives an authorization token from eBay and does not receive the seller’s eBay password.

How we use information

We use the information only to support the seller’s inventory workflow, including finding records by SKU, preparing or updating draft inventory, and attaching product photos. RTRD Inventory does not automatically publish a listing without a separate authorized action.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate and secure the application, or when required by law.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens are encrypted at rest, and access is limited to authorized staff. We retain information only as long as needed for operations, security, legal obligations, and reasonable error recovery.

Your choices

You may revoke the application’s access through your eBay account permissions. For privacy questions or requests, contact ktango503@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the application changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
